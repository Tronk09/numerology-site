Cookie Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember information about your visit and are commonly used to make sites work and to measure how they are used.

2. Cookies we use

Our site uses analytics tools to understand how visitors use the site — which pages are read, how long people stay, and what works. This helps us keep improving the site. The tools we use and the cookies they set are:

ProviderPurposeApprox. persistence
Google Analytics 4Site usage analytics — pages, sessions, approximate location, device.Up to 2 years
Microsoft ClaritySession recordings and heatmaps to see how the site is used and where visitors struggle.About 1 year

In addition, our site may use the So-called technical cookies needed for the site to display and function correctly (for example, to remember your currency/region preference), and cookies set by any third-party services we rely on, such as our form processor.

3. Why we use them

We use these cookies to understand how the site performs so we can make it more useful and easier to use. This is a legitimate interest of ours. We do not use cookies to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell any data collected by cookies.

4. Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings — most browsers let you block or remove them. You can also opt out of analytics directly at the providers’ tools:

  • Google Analytics opt-out: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Microsoft Clarity: you can disable Clarity tracking for your visits within your browser, and you can learn more at clarity.microsoft.com.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect how the site works for you, but it will still be fully readable.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy or our use of cookies, contact us at hello@getninefold.com.
